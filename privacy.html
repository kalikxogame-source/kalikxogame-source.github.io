Privacy Policy

Kalikxo Uploader is a personal desktop tool that publishes the owner's own animated videos to the owner's own TikTok account and YouTube channel through the official TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube API Services.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. Data the app accesses

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): your TikTok display name and open ID, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Posting permissions (video.publish, video.upload): used only to upload the videos you queued and read their publishing status.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after you approve the connection.
  • YouTube (Google) account: the app uses YouTube API Services. With the scope youtube.upload it uploads the owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube channel as Shorts; with youtube.readonly it reads only the channel name to confirm which channel is connected. It stores the OAuth refresh token Google issues. It does not read, collect, or change any other YouTube or Google data.

2. Where the data is stored

The tokens, the list of videos with captions, and a log of what was published are kept in the app's folder on the owner's computer. For scheduled publishing while the computer is off, the same tokens are also kept in the owner's own private GitHub repository, encrypted (PBKDF2 + HMAC-SHA256), and are decrypted only during the owner's scheduled job. The app has no public server and no database of its own.

3. Who receives the data

The app communicates only with TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com) and Google's official APIs (oauth2.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com) to log in and upload the owner's videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

By using the YouTube features you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

4. How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until they expire, until you revoke access in TikTok or Google, or until you delete the app's folder and the private repository. Deleting them removes all data the app stored.

5. Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Revoke YouTube/Google access at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • Delete the files tokeny.json and youtube_tokeny.json to remove the stored tokens immediately.
  • Questions: kalikxogame@gmail.com.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone. It is used only by its owner with their own account.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
