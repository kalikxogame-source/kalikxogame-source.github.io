Terms of Service

Effective date: November 2, 2026

1. What the app does

Kalikxo Uploader runs on the owner's own Windows computer. It uploads video files that the owner created, together with their captions and hashtags, to the TikTok account that the owner connected through TikTok Login Kit. It can post directly or send the video to the TikTok inbox as a draft, according to the owner's settings.

2. Who may use it

The app is intended for use by its owner with their own TikTok account. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it does not post on behalf of other people.

3. Your content

You must own, or have the rights to, every video and caption you publish with the app. You remain responsible for your content and for following the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. Acceptable use

  • Do not use the app to post spam, misleading content, or content you do not have rights to.
  • Do not try to bypass TikTok's posting limits or privacy settings.
  • Do not share your app keys or access tokens with anyone.

5. Availability

The app is provided as is, without warranties. Publishing depends on TikTok's services, which may change, limit, or reject uploads. The developer is not responsible for content that TikTok removes or for interruptions in TikTok's services.

6. Disconnecting

You can stop using the app at any time by turning off its scheduled task and revoking its access in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

7. Changes

These terms may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

1. Data the app accesses

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): your TikTok display name and open ID, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Posting permissions (video.publish, video.upload): used only to upload the videos you queued and read their publishing status.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after you approve the connection.

2. Where the data is stored

Everything stays on your own computer, in the app's folder: the tokens, the list of videos with captions, and a log of what was published. The app has no server and no database of its own.

3. Who receives the data

The app communicates only with TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com) to log in and upload your videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

4. How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until they expire, until you revoke access in TikTok, or until you delete the app's folder. Deleting the folder removes all data the app stored.

5. Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Delete the file tokeny.json to remove the stored tokens immediately.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone. It is used only by its owner with their own account.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
