1. What the app does

Kalikxo Uploader runs on the owner's own Windows computer. It uploads video files that the owner created, together with their captions and hashtags, to the TikTok account that the owner connected through TikTok Login Kit. It can post directly or send the video to the TikTok inbox as a draft, according to the owner's settings. It can also upload the same videos as Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel through YouTube API Services.

2. Who may use it

The app is intended for use by its owner with their own TikTok account. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it does not post on behalf of other people.
